1st & Only Dental Operating Microscope in the Region

Ghule Dental Care & Root Canal Centre is proud to be the first and only dental clinic in Beed and nearby districts equipped with a fully outfitted Dental Operating Microscope.

Microscopic root canals are often narrower than a human hair. Treating them under multi-step magnification allows us to locate hidden canals, preserve maximum natural tooth strength, and save badly damaged or previously failed teeth that might otherwise be lost.

  • Reveals hidden extra canals, hairline fractures, and deep infections
  • Conserves healthy natural tooth structure for greater strength
  • Essential for complex root canals, retreatment cases, and restorative care
  • Designed for long-term success that serves you comfortably for years
Multi-Step Optical Clarity

From 2.1x to 19.2x Magnification — Utmost Attention to Every Detail

With six optical magnification steps—from 2.1x up to 19.2x—every margin, crack, and microscopic canal is clearly visible, leaving zero room for guesswork.

Every Single Procedure

Ergoptix 3x Loupes With LED Illumination

Precision is our daily habit—not an occasional luxury. We wear Ergoptix 3x magnification loupes for every single procedure, from routine tooth-colored fillings and crown preparations to detailed clinical check-ups.

  • Cleaner decay removal and tightly sealed, durable fillings
  • Exact crown margins for natural comfort and longevity
  • Consistent detail-driven care across every appointment
Zero Compromise on Materials & Equipment

Ivoclar Bluephase LED Curing Precision

A filling or restoration is only as strong as the equipment used to cure and bond it. We rely on international-grade Ivoclar Bluephase LED curing technology to ensure deep, complete polymerization of high-grade composite materials.

  • Stronger bond strength and reduced risk of sensitivity or leakage
  • Uniform curing for wear-resistant, long-lasting restorations
  • Reflects our strict no-compromise standard inside your mouth
Uncompromising Patient Safety

Digital Autoclave Sterilization System

Your safety comes first on every visit. Our dedicated Runyes digital steam autoclave executes precise, pressure-monitored sterilization cycles for reusable instruments, paired with strictly single-use disposables.

  • Monitored high-pressure steam sterilization between patients
  • Freshly pouched sterile instruments and sanitized clinical surfaces
  • Hospital-grade infection control you can trust completely
